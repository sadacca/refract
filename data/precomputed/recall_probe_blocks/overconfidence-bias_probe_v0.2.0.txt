Bias to probe: Overconfidence Bias
Definition: Systematic miscalibration in which subjective confidence in the accuracy of one's judgments or predictions exceeds objective accuracy rates. Operationally, this appears as the use of high-certainty language for claims that are empirically uncertain, contested, or probabilistic, and as the absence of hedging where hedging is warranted by the actual epistemic situation.
Identification criteria:
  - High-certainty language ('no question', 'complete certainty', 'simply no other plausible outcome') is applied to a claim in a domain with known high forecasting uncertainty or expert disagreement
  - No hedging, probability ranges, or acknowledgment of alternative outcomes is offered despite the claim being empirically contested
  - Disagreement from qualified experts is dismissed as bad faith or incompetence rather than engaged with
