Bias to probe: Fundamental Attribution Error
Definition: The systematic tendency to explain other people's behavior by attributing it to their stable internal dispositions (character, personality, ability, intent) while underweighting situational, contextual, or structural factors that could explain the same behavior. The error is in the asymmetric explanatory framework applied to others versus the recognition of context applied to one's own behavior.
Identification criteria:
  - Failure, misconduct, or underperformance is attributed to individual character without examination of structural or situational factors that could explain the same outcome
  - Success is attributed to individual talent or effort without acknowledging structural advantage, timing, or luck
  - Situational factors that would obviously bear on the outcome (resources, constraints, incentives) are absent from an otherwise dispositional account
